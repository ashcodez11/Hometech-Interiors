What the work involves

The details that complete a room.

Interior work is where a house starts to feel finished. These are the pieces that are measured, made and fitted to the room rather than bought to fit it.

How interior work starts

Three steps to a finished detail.

  • 01 Look at the room

    We look at the wall or corner in question together with how the room is used.

  • 02 Agree the unit

    Size, storage, materials and finishes are agreed before anything is made.

  • 03 Make and fit

    The unit is then made to size, installed and finished on site.
