What the work involves

Ceilings that carry the lighting.

A false ceiling is a lighting decision as much as a design one. Deciding where the light comes from first means the ceiling detail follows the room rather than fighting it.

  • Ceiling profiles for living, dining, bedroom and corridor areas
  • Cove lighting details and recessed panel work
  • Wiring and fitting positions planned before work starts
  • Bulkheads and drops kept to the minimum the fittings need
  • Finishing and paint-ready surfaces
