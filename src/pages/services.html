Interior services in Chennai

Services

Four core areas of interior work — modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings and interior works — carried out from our base in Saidapet, Chennai.

01

Modular Kitchens

A kitchen works when the layout follows the way the room is actually used. We plan base and wall units, tall units, worktop runs and storage together so that appliances, vessels and daily use items each have a place.

  • Layouts planned around available measurements and plumbing positions
  • Base units, wall units, tall units and loft storage
  • Cutlery, bottle and appliance storage worked into the layout
  • Shutters, worktops and handles chosen with you

02

Wardrobes

Wardrobes are fitted to the room rather than bought to a size. Internal planning covers hanging sections at the right heights, drawer stacks, shelving and loft storage for seasonal items.

  • Fitted to the available width, height and depth, including awkward corners
  • Hanging, shelving, drawer and loft sections planned together
  • Internal drawers and organisers for smaller items
  • Shutter finishes and hardware chosen to suit the room

03

False Ceilings

Ceiling work does more than change the look of a room. It carries wiring, locates light fittings and can hide services — which is why the lighting layout is decided at the same time as the ceiling design.

  • Ceiling profiles for living, dining, bedroom and corridor areas
  • Cove lighting details and recessed panel work
  • Wiring and fitting positions planned before work starts
  • Ceiling height considered so rooms do not feel lowered

04

Interior Works

The finishing layer of a home: furniture, partitions, display and pooja units, study areas and the small details that make separate rooms feel like one interior.

  • Television, crockery and display units
  • Room partitions and screens with storage
  • Pooja units and study areas planned to fit the room
  • Finishing and detailing across rooms

Before you enquire

Questions we are usually asked.

How do I start a project with Hometech Interiors?

Call or WhatsApp us on {{site.phoneDisplay}} with a short description of the space and what you would like done, or send the enquiry form on the contact page. We will take it from there.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Saidapet, Chennai and work with homes and spaces across the city. If you are unsure whether your location works, just ask when you get in touch.

Can you handle more than one part of the work?

Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings and interior works are all part of what we do, and they are often easier to coordinate together — particularly when a ceiling has to accommodate lighting above a kitchen or dining area.

Do you give a price over the phone?

Costs depend on measurements, materials and the scope of the work, so they are worked out after the space has been discussed. Share your requirements and we will tell you what information we need.
