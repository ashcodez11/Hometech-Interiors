Before you enquire

Questions we are usually asked.

How do I start a project with Hometech Interiors?

Call or WhatsApp us on {{site.phoneDisplay}} with a short description of the space and what you would like done, or send the enquiry form on the contact page. We will take it from there.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Saidapet, Chennai and work with homes and spaces across the city. If you are unsure whether your location works, just ask when you get in touch.

Can you handle more than one part of the work?

Yes. Interior works, kitchens, wardrobes, TV and pooja units, false ceilings, wallpaper and complete interiors are all part of what we do, and they are often easier to coordinate together.

Do you give a price over the phone?

Costs depend on measurements, materials and the scope of the work, so they are worked out after the space has been discussed. Share your requirements and we will tell you what information we need.
