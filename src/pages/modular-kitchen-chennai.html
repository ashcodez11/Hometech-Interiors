What the work involves

A kitchen planned around the room.

No two kitchens in Chennai are the same size, and the position of plumbing, windows and doors decides most of the layout. Planning starts there.

  • Base units, wall units and tall units sized to the room
  • Worktop runs positioned around the sink and cooking area
  • Drawer and cutlery storage, plus dedicated space for larger vessels
  • Loft storage above the units for occasional use items
  • Shutter finishes, handles and hardware discussed with you
