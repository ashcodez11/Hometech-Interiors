Useful to know

Before you call.

What should I have ready when I get in touch?

The room you want to work on, roughly what you would like done, and any photographs or measurements you already have. None of it is compulsory — it simply helps the conversation move faster.

Which services can I ask about?

Modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings and interior works. If your requirement spans more than one of these, mention it — they are often planned together.

What happens after I send the form?

On this demonstration site the form prepares your enquiry as a WhatsApp message so nothing is stored on a server. On the live site the enquiry would reach Hometech Interiors directly.
