What the work involves

Storage planned to the room, not to a size.

Wardrobe space is usually the first thing a home runs short of. Fitting it to the wall, floor to ceiling, is what makes the difference.

  • Fitted to the available width, height and depth, including corners
  • Hanging sections at long and short lengths
  • Drawers, organisers and shelving for folded and smaller items
  • Loft storage for seasonal and occasional items
  • Shutter styles and hardware discussed with you

How a wardrobe project starts

Three steps to fitted storage.

  • 01 Measure the room

    Wall lengths, ceiling height, beams, switches and skirting all affect what can be fitted.

  • 02 Plan the interior

    Hanging, drawers, shelving and loft space are divided up according to what you store.

  • 03 Execute the work

    Carcass, shutters, internals and hardware are then installed and finished.
