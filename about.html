About Hometech Interiors

Interior decorators and false ceiling contractors in Saidapet, Chennai — 22 years in business, working on modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings and interior works.

Who we are

An interior practice built on practical work.

Hometech Interiors is based in Saidapet, Chennai and provides interior solutions including modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings and interior works.

Interior work in Chennai is site work. Every home brings its own measurements, its own light and its own set of constraints, so the work starts with understanding the space rather than with a catalogue. The services we offer are the ones we have delivered for years: kitchen layouts, fitted wardrobes, ceiling work and the interior details that complete a room.

Where a space needs coordination — a false ceiling that has to hide wiring for lights above a dining table, or a wardrobe that has to fit around an existing beam — it helps to have a single team handling the pieces together.

How it works

From first conversation to finished room.

A simple sequence, kept deliberately short so you always know what happens next.

  • 01 Understand the space

    We talk about the room, how you want to use it and what you already have. Measurements and site conditions come first.

  • 02 Plan the details

    Layouts, storage, materials and finishes are worked through together, so decisions are made before work begins.

  • 03 Carry out the work

    Kitchen, wardrobe, ceiling and interior work is then executed as one coordinated job rather than separate pieces.
